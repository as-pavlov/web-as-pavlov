Tanya rescues the cats — Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile game "Tanya rescues the cats" (the "App") handles information about its users. The App is developed and published by AS-Pavlov (Andrey Pavlov) ("I", "we").

Children's audience

This App is designed for children. It is built and operated in compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the Google Play Families Policy, and the EU GDPR rules for children.

Information I collect

I do not directly collect, store or process any personally identifiable information (PII) on my own servers. The App does not contain user accounts, does not ask the user for their name, email, phone number or any other personal data, and does not contain user-generated content shared with other users.

Android Advertising ID

The App does NOT request the Android Advertising ID (AD_ID). The corresponding permission is explicitly removed from the App's manifest. As a result, advertising shown in the App is contextual (non-personalized) only.

Local data on the device

The App stores game progress, settings and similar information in the local storage of your device. This data never leaves your device. To delete it, uninstall the App or use Android: Settings → Apps → Tanya rescues the cats → Storage → Clear data.

In-app purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. I do not see your payment details — they are handled entirely by Google. To request a refund or manage purchases, use your Google Account.

Third-party advertising SDKs

To monetise the App, the following third-party advertising SDKs are integrated. These SDKs may collect non-personal technical information (such as device model, OS version, IP address, ad performance data) to serve contextual advertisements. They do not receive personal data from me. Each SDK is configured for child-directed (COPPA-compliant) mode where applicable.

  • IronSource LevelPlay — mediation platform with bidders ironSource, Unity Ads, InMobi, Yandex bidder. Privacy Policy
  • Yandex Mobile Ads — uses AppMetrica internally for attribution and anti-fraud. Privacy Policy
  • Kidoz — kid-safe contextual-only advertising network. Privacy Policy
  • TopOn — advertising mediation platform. Privacy Policy
  • Tradplus — advertising mediation platform. Privacy Policy

Permissions

The App requests only the standard network permissions required for advertising delivery (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE).

Data deletion and parental rights

Because I do not store any personal data on my side, there is nothing for me to delete on my servers. To remove all local data on the device, uninstall the App.

If you are a parent and want me to coordinate a data-deletion request to one of the third-party advertising networks listed above, please email me. I will forward the request to the corresponding network's privacy team. Per GDPR I will respond within 30 days.
